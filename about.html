KANUKA TELUGU DAILY

ABOUT

Kanuka Telugu Daily was founded in 2014 by Mallikarjuna Rao S with a steadfast commitment to delivering reliable news and enriching content to the Telugu-speaking community. Since its establishment, Kanuka Telugu Daily has strived to provide comprehensive coverage across diverse topics such as politics, entertainment, sports, business, and more.

Under the guidance of Mallikarjuna Rao S, Kanuka Telugu Daily has become a trusted name in Telugu journalism, known for its integrity, accuracy, and dedication to public service. The newspaper aims to keep its readers informed and engaged through both its print and digital platforms, ensuring accessibility and relevance in today's fast-paced media environment. Driven by a passion for journalism and a deep-rooted sense of community, Kanuka Telugu Daily continues to evolve, adapting to the evolving needs of its audience while upholding the highest standards of journalistic ethics. Mallikarjuna Rao S's leadership has been instrumental in shaping the newspaper's identity as a beacon of reliable information and a catalyst for social awareness among Telugu-speaking readers.
